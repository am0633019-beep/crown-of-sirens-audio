Songs of the Eternal Oath · Book One

Crown of Sirens

Written and read by Adam Martin

You asked me, in the yard, why I never talk about it. The honest answer is that I have been putting it off for eleven years and running out of reasons.

What follows is Elion's account of the year the sand turned. Set down as truthfully as one man can, eleven years on — with all the qualifications that entails.
